# NEXORA Scope Studio — Project Brief

## Product concept

A lightweight customer discovery tool that helps NEXORA AI turn an early idea into a structured, budget-aware first-release plan before a formal proposal is prepared.

## Why this project

A customer may know the outcome they want but not know which features are essential, how large the first release should be, or whether the requested scope fits their planned budget. This tool makes those conversations more structured and helps avoid promising work before the scope is understood.

## MVP scope

- Four project categories: websites, mobile apps, AI solutions, and automation.
- Inputs for business goal, scope size, design preference, features, integrations, budget, and preferred timeline.
- A simple transparent INR estimate model.
- Budget-fit guidance with an MVP-first suggestion.
- Exportable plain-text project brief.
- Responsive accessible interface.

## Out of scope for this version

- Customer accounts or authentication.
- Server-side storage or CRM integration.
- Payments, contracts, or automatic quotations.
- Automatic emails, customer messaging, or project creation.
- Live vendor/API pricing.

## Success criteria

- A visitor can complete the form on a phone or desktop.
- The estimate responds to selected scope and feature changes.
- A visitor can generate, copy, and download a brief.
- No form data is sent to a server.
- The page runs as a static site without paid services or environment variables.

## Commercial guardrails

The estimate is indicative only. NEXORA AI must confirm requirements, dependencies, support expectations, delivery milestones, and any third-party charges before issuing a final written quote. No paid service should be purchased or activated without the required customer and account-owner approval.
